# 3. AI Usage Note

I used **ChatGPT** at a few stages of the project as a support tool.

### Where I used AI

- I used it for some **initial ideas for the dashboard structure**, such as showing order status, priority orders, inventory issues, exceptions, and courier pickups.
- While developing the Streamlit app, I used ChatGPT for **small coding suggestions and debugging**, especially when I faced Streamlit warnings or UI/code issues.
- I also used it to create and review some **sample/dummy data** so that the different fulfillment scenarios could be demonstrated.
- I used AI for some **UI improvement ideas**, but I selected and adjusted the changes based on what I felt was practical and easy to understand.

### Where I changed or disagreed with AI suggestions

 **Dashboard design:** I tried a more heavily redesigned UI suggested during development, but I felt it made the application less simple. I went back toward the original, simpler dashboard structure and only kept a few visual improvements.
